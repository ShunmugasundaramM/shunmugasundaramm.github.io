What owners say today

Seven complaints from app reviews, seven screens that answer them

Remote commands fail with no reason given

Three-stage status: Sent, Reached your car, Done, with the reason in plain words

Sign-in loops on OTP or shows “No Records Found”

One sign-in that finds the car by registration number, with chat help on the same screen

Renewal dead-ends on a blank payment page

UPI renewal with a 48-hour hold, so a failed payment never loses the plan

Fees appear after you commit

“You pay at most ₹3,750” on the estimate; extra work needs your approval

Support always ends at a phone number

In-app support chat that already knows your car and the error

Car health never reaches service booking

Odometer and health check attached to the booking automatically

Data use is never explained

A plain-words data summary, and workshop access that ends on its own

The product idea

A free care layer for every car, a premium layer for connected ones

Free · every Maruti car

Care layer

  • Service booking and live workshop status
  • Insurance, PUC and RC with day counters
  • Documents, challan and FASTag status
  • Roadside help, SOS and recall notices

Premium · cars with the device

Car layer

  • Remote lock, climate and find my car
  • Live location and geofence
  • Trips and driving score
  • Alert history

Nothing free today moves behind the paywall. Safety stays free.

Under the hood

Each status the owner sees maps to a hop the command makes

If a command stops between the network and the car, the app can say the car has no signal instead of “something went wrong”. This is a simplified, generic path from public information. I'd confirm the real architecture and failure codes with the telematics team.

If I join

First 90 days: owners before pixels

The concept is built on reviews, not owners. The first job is to test it against real people and real data.

  1. WEEKS 1–4

    Listen

    Walk through today's apps with 5–8 owners, with and without the device. Read support tickets and dealer feedback.

  2. WEEKS 5–8

    Test

    Usability-test the lock-failure and service-approval flows. Map the command path with the telematics team.

  3. WEEKS 9–12

    Measure

    A/B test one change in the live app, such as plain-words command errors, and report the result.
