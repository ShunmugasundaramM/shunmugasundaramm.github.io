Problem Statement

Users face a significant lack of investment knowledge, hindering their confidence in financial decisions. Switching between multiple apps for various purposes, such as news updates and stock transactions, poses a notable challenge. Analyzing market trends and deciphering buy/sell signals is a common struggle. Insufficient knowledge about investments leads to hesitancy among potential investors. Real-time expert consultation is absent, leaving users without immediate guidance. Past investors suffered losses due to outdated information and holding de-listed stocks. High brokerage fees and the uncertainty of charges post-trade add to the user's concerns. Addressing these issues is crucial for enhancing user experience and fostering a more informed and confident investment community.

Solution Offering

Empower users with a comprehensive educational module within the app to bridge the investment knowledge gap. Integrate a unified platform for news updates and seamless stock transactions. Implement an AI-driven tool for simplified market trend analysis and real-time buy/sell signal identification. Offer educational resources to boost investor confidence. Introduce a live expert consultation feature for immediate guidance. Ensure timely updates on stock statuses to prevent losses. Minimize brokerage fees and provide transparent cost previews pre-trade. By addressing these aspects, our enhanced trading app aims to cultivate a knowledgeable, confident, and thriving investment community.

Understanding Requirements

Before initiating the survey, I engaged in one-on-one discussions with the stakeholders. This facilitated a comprehensive understanding of their perspectives and thoughts, allowing them to articulate responses to a predefined set of specific questions.

During the survey,

Through the survey, it was affirmed that the primary challenge faced by users is a lack of knowledge about the stock market. This finding aligns consistently with the insights gathered during interviews. The key issues identified include:

01.

Confusion between Trading & Investment

Users struggle to distinguish between trading and investment, indicating a need for clarity in these concepts.

02.

Limited Knowledge about Stock Brokers

Users lack information about stock brokers, highlighting a potential hurdle in selecting suitable platforms for their financial activities.

03.

Limited Understanding of Stock Exchanges

A significant portion of users lacks understanding regarding how stock exchanges operate, indicating a need for educational resources on this topic.

04.

Inadequate Awareness of Market Analysis

Users face challenges in analyzing market trends, emphasizing the necessity for tools or guidance in market analysis.

05.

Uncertainty in Stock Selection:

Users express hesitation in choosing stocks, underscoring a need for resources that provide insights into effective stock selection strategies.

By addressing these user problems, the trading and investment platform can enhance its user experience and empower users with the knowledge needed for informed financial decisions.

User personas

Three archetypes drawn from 2026 market data and from recurring themes in public posts and press coverage. Names and details are composites, not real people.

ASThe SIP starter

Aditi Sharma

24 · Indore · First job, IT services · Investing for 8 monthsBased on: Under-30s are 59% of new registrations and 1 in 4 investors is a woman (NSE, 2026). Average SIP ticket is about ₹3,000 (AMFI, Aug 2026).

Goals

  • Start a ₹2,000 SIP and not have to think about it
  • Understand what she owns without a finance degree
  • Build an emergency fund before anything risky

Frustrations

  • Every fund page shows twenty numbers and no answer
  • Friends forward stock tips on WhatsApp she can’t verify
  • Doesn’t know if a red day means she did something wrong

What Pocket gives them

A plain first-fund path, SIPs from ₹500, and a portfolio that explains a down day in one line.

RVThe weekly options trader

Rohit Verma

29 · Lucknow · Runs a mobile accessories shop · Trades weekly optionsBased on: 91% of individual F&O traders lost money in FY25, averaging ₹1.1 lakh each (SEBI). Paid Telegram “tip” channels and outage complaints on X are recurring themes in coverage.

Goals

  • Make back last quarter’s losses
  • Place orders fast on expiry day
  • Pay less in charges

Frustrations

  • The app froze during a volatile session and he missed his exit
  • Hidden charges only show up in the contract note
  • Tip channels promise returns that never come

What Pocket gives them

His real P&L after charges, a status banner during outages, and a pause before every options order that shows the odds.

SKThe careful long-term saver

Suresh Kumar

52 · Coimbatore · Textile trader · Moving money out of FDsBased on: Individuals now hold 18.6% of NSE-listed market cap, up from 14.6% five years ago (NSE). Gold ETF assets rose 163% in a year (AMFI, Aug 2026).

Goals

  • Earn more than his fixed deposits without losing sleep
  • Hold gold and a few large companies
  • Make sure his family can claim everything

Frustrations

  • Small grey text and crowded screens
  • Jargon like “MTF” and “pledge” without explanation
  • Worries about fake apps and calls that impersonate brokers

What Pocket gives them

Larger text, plain English with every term explained, a nominee set up at sign-up, and a clear sign the app is the real one.

HMW Diagram

To understand the information archietecture and the user flow, I created the HMW diagram to evaluate consumer feelings and requirements in an app.

How Might We
1. How Might We… Improve Educational Accessibility?
2.How Might We… Simplify Decision-Making Processes?
3. How Might We… Enhance User Confidence in Stock Selection?
4. How Might We… Provide Real-Time Guidance for Users?
5. How Might We… Clarify Distinctions Between Trading and Investment?
6. How Might We… Ensure Transparent Fee Structures?
7. How Might We… Encourage Consistent Investing Habits?
8. How Might We… Address Urgent Cash Flow Requirements Without Selling Shares?
Challenge
Users expressed a desire for more educational resources
Users struggle with complexities in financial decision-making
Users feel hesitant and uncertain when selecting stocks
Users expressed a need for real-time expert consultation
Users find it challenging to differentiate between trading and investment
Users desire clarity on fees and charges
Users lack the discipline or habit to save money every month
Users face the need to sell shares for urgent cash flow
Solution
Implement a dedicated section with comprehensive educational modules accessible from the main interface
Develop tools or features that provide step-by-step guidance, simplifying the decision-making process
Introduce features or resources that guide effective stock selection and provide relevant insights
Implement a feature for live expert consultations or real-time support, enhancing user guidance
Clearly distinguish and label trading and investment features within the app to avoid confusion
Provide a clear and transparent breakdown of fees, including a preview of charges pre-trade
Implement features that encourage and reward consistent investing, possibly through reminders or incentives
Introduce features or options for short-term liquidity without necessarily selling long-term investments

By addressing these HMW questions, the app can focus on specific areas to enhance the overall user experience, aligning with consumer feelings and requirements.
