The flows people tested

Each flow below is in the prototype and was part of the test. They run in the order a new investor meets them, from opening an account to moving money.

Ten steps to an account, one per screen

Mobile number, DigiLocker, a ₹1 UPI bank check and Aadhaar eSign. A shorter KYC that saves as you go.

Every price is one tap deep

A live price, a chart you can drag across to read any day, and the day’s range at a glance.

  • Live NSE price
  • Drag to scrub the chart
  • Hear chart as a list
  • Day and 52-week range

An option chain you can read

Switch between indices and MCX, read OI or the Greeks, and build a strategy straight from the chain.

  • Indices and MCX in one list
  • OI and Greeks as tabs
  • Build a strategy from the chain
  • The SEBI risk line, up front

Say what it costs before it costs

MTF shows interest per day before you buy. Order types explain themselves. A waiting order says how far the price is from filling.

Funds and SIPs without the jargon

Browse by collection, read a fund’s three-year story on one chart, and see what a monthly SIP could become.

Exit fast, stop when you need to

Exit all sits at the top of Positions. The kill switch pauses trading for a day, a week or until you say.

IPOs and research calls, losses included

An IPO timeline you can read at a glance, and research calls that show the full track record.

Know what you have, move money simply

Returns after charges, with XIRR beside them. Cash, collateral and where every rupee moved today.

Long screens and two themes

The long screens are where the hierarchy gets tested: Home, a stock, a fund and the SIP calculator, top to bottom. The prototype also runs in light.

What testing found

Eight personas, built from 2026 NSE, SEBI and AMFI figures, each worked through their own tasks. This was a simulated expert walkthrough, not sessions with real people.
