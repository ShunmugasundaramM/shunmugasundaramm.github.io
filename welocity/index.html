01 · The cover

A wave of light across a black field.

Front and back share one continuous wave that runs from amber to violet, drawn as a fine dot field. The yellow arrow inside the wordmark's "o" is the only hard colour on the cover, so the title reads first from across a room.

02 · Contents and opener

Twenty-one articles, one index.

The contents page sets each article as a large page number with a yellow rule, the title, and the author with their role, so readers can find a fund house or a name at a glance. It faces the opening feature by Wealthy's co-founders, The Great Indian Wealth Management Opportunity.

03 · Product pages

The year's releases, on dark spreads.

Product pages run on black fading to violet, with app screens floating on soft circles of light. Each feature gets a short heading with a yellow underline and two or three lines of copy: new client listing, Learn with Wealthy, AIF reporting, SIP features, Smart Search, the Earnings Dashboard and SmartValues stock baskets.

04 · Partner stories

A lighter page for people.

Partner success stories switch to light pages so they read as a different voice. Each one opens with a portrait in a yellow frame and a pull quote on a dark band, then the partner's own account under recurring headings, so stories can be compared side by side.

Two page systems, one volume.

Dark spreads for product and company news, light spreads for partners and fund houses, held together by the same type, yellow accents and folio.
