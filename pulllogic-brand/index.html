Logo

Structure

The PullLogic logo combines a modular “P” symbol with a refined wordmark. The symbol represents structured flow and connected systems. The typography adds authority and clarity. Both elements work together to create a balanced, scalable identity.

Clear space

A minimum clear space of X must surround the logo at all times. X equals the height of one square unit in the symbol. No elements may enter this protected area.

X

Variants and backgrounds

Use the primary logo on light backgrounds and the reversed logo on dark backgrounds. Avoid busy or low-contrast surfaces.

Logo on image. Use the logo on imagery to strengthen brand visibility, with clear contrast and enough space. Do not distort, recolor or overcrowd the logo.

Use prohibited

App icon

A simplified mark for digital platforms. Use only approved backgrounds. Do not alter color, shape or proportions.

Partner logo lockup

A structured dividing space separates the two identities, balanced in height, weight and alignment. All spacing is defined from P, the height of the capital “P”, with 1× P of clear space around the full lockup.
